Execution rules

BUY/SELL on a newly closed candle (4/5 or 5/5). Entry is simulated at the next candle open. Stop uses the scanner level. Exit fully at POC if it offers ≥1.5R, otherwise at the VA edge if it qualifies. One position at a time. Maximum three entries or three losses per IST day.

Prices are futures contract prices. Candle simulation assumes stop first when both exits are touched. Gaps can increase losses. P&L excludes fees, funding and slippage. Keep this page open for new entries. Saved positions reconcile against available candle history after reopening.

    Delta
    Taker buy volume se taker sell volume ghatao. +20 BTC ka matlab buyers ne sellers se 20 BTC zyada aggressively trade kiya.
    

    CVD
    Har candle ka signed delta jodne se cumulative volume delta milta hai. Scanner sirf aakhri 3 closed candles ka sum dekhta hai.
    

    Imbalance
    Kisi price level par aggressive buying ya selling bhaari ho sakti hai. Poori candle ka delta us level ki pehchaan nahi batata.
    

    POC / VAH / VAL
    Har candle ka volume uski range ke bins mein barabar baantte hain. Sabse zyada estimated volume wala bin POC hai. Wahan se 70% volume cover karne wali area ki upper aur lower boundaries VAH aur VAL hain.
    

    Absorption
    Heavy buying ke bawajood price aage na badhe toh passive sellers buys absorb kar rahe ho sakte hain. Yeh observation hai, scanner ka signal rule nahi.
    

    Signal kaise banta hai?
    Band candle par VAL/VAH test, delta, 3-candle CVD, volume aur close ki position check hoti hai. Leading side ke 4–5 points = BUY/SELL, 3 = WATCH, baaki WAIT. Barabari par WAIT.
    

    Risk rules
    Ek trade mein maximum 1% risk. Minimum R:R 1:1.5 dekho. Din mein maximum 3 trades lo. Teen losses ke baad us din trading band karo.
    

    Footprint ki limit
    Scanner mein delta candle-level hai. Order Flow tab par observed trades se per-price footprint banta hai. Asli per-price footprint ke liye aggTrades ya trade WebSocket data ko price aur taker side ke hisaab se group karna padta hai.
    

    DOM aur liquidation
    DOM mein pending limit orders hain, jo cancel ho sakte hain. Tape mein executed trades hain. Liquidation map mein exchange se mile forced fills dikhte hain. Blank interval mein feed incomplete bhi ho sakti hai.
    

    OI aur funding
    OI khule contracts ki quantity hai. Funding perpetual contract ki periodic payment rate hai. OI badhne se akela long ya short direction pata nahi chalta.
    

    Footprint kaise padhein?
    VAH, VAL ya POC ke paas bid/ask rows dekho. Teen adjacent imbalances ek potential zone hain. Heavy selling ke baad price upar close kare toh absorption mumkin hai. Agli candles se level hold hua ya nahi dekho.
    

    Coverage, VWAP aur TPO
    Order Flow latest aggregate prints se shuru hota hai. Session ya anchor purana ho toh sirf available trades count hote hain. VWAP volume-weighted average price hai. TPO ek price row par alag 30-minute periods ginta hai.
